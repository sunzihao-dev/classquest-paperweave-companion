Working vocabulary

Encoder: represents input text. Decoder: produces an output sequence. Retriever: selects relevant passages. Generator: produces an answer. Checkpoint: saved model weights. Initialize: choose starting weights. Fine-tune: update weights through training. Scope: the conditions under which a claim applies.
